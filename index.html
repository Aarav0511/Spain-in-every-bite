Entrantes

Starters

Patatas bravas is a traditional and popular Spanish tapas dish made from crispy, golden-fried potato cubes served with a spicy tomato-based bravas sauce, often accompanied by creamy aioli, giving it a delicious combination of crispy, spicy, and creamy flavours.

Pan con tomate is a traditional and simple Spanish dish from Catalonia, made with toasted bread rubbed with ripe tomatoes and garlic, then drizzled with olive oil and sprinkled with salt, giving it a fresh, savoury, and delicious flavour.

A quesadilla is a popular Mexican dish consisting of a tortilla—usually flour or corn—filled primarily with melted cheese. It is folded in half into a half-moon shape and cooked on a griddle until the outside is crispy and golden and the inside is gooey.

Nachos con guacamole is a popular and tasty dish made with crispy tortilla chips served with creamy guacamole prepared from ripe avocados, lime juice, tomatoes, onions, and herbs, giving it a fresh, crunchy, and flavourful taste.

Plato Principal

Main Course

Vegetable paella is a traditional and colourful Spanish rice dish made with fresh vegetables, rice, saffron, and flavourful spices, all cooked together in a wide pan, giving it a rich, aromatic, and delicious taste.

Tacos are a popular and flavourful Mexican dish made with soft or crispy tortillas filled with vegetables, beans, cheese, salsa, and other toppings. They offer a delicious combination of fresh, spicy, and savoury flavours and can be easily customized with different fillings.

Pimientos de Padrón is a traditional Spanish tapas dish from Galicia, made with small green peppers fried in olive oil and sprinkled with sea salt. They have a mild, slightly smoky flavour, although occasionally you may find a surprisingly spicy one!

Postres

Desserts

Churros are a popular Spanish sweet treat made from fried dough that is crispy on the outside and soft on the inside, often coated with sugar and cinnamon. They are commonly served warm with thick hot chocolate for dipping, making them a delicious and comforting dessert.

Bebidas

Drinks

Horchata de chufa is a traditional and refreshing Spanish drink from Valencia, made with soaked and ground tiger nuts, water, and sugar. It has a sweet, creamy, and slightly nutty flavour and is usually served cold, making it especially refreshing on hot days.

Agua de València is a refreshing and popular Spanish drink from Valencia, made with fresh orange juice and other flavourful ingredients. It has a sweet, citrusy, and vibrant taste and is usually served chilled, making it a refreshing drink for celebrations and special occasions.
